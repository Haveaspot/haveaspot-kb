Haveaspot may update its Terms of Service from time to time. Here is how we handle changes and how they affect you.

## How We Notify You

If we make **material changes** to the Terms, we will notify you in advance — for example:

- By email to the address associated with your account
- By notification on the Platform

We will give you reasonable advance notice before material changes take effect — typically at least **30 days**.

## What Counts as a Material Change?

Material changes include updates to the fee structure, cancellation policy, or other financial terms, as well as significant changes to your rights or obligations. Minor editorial updates or clarifications may not require advance notice.

## Your Continued Use Means Acceptance

If you continue to use the Platform after the effective date of updated Terms, you are deemed to have accepted the revised Terms. If you do not agree with the changes, you should stop using the Platform.

## Changes Do Not Apply Retroactively to Existing Bookings

Any changes to fees, the Cancellation Policy, or other financial terms apply **strictly to Bookings confirmed after the effective date** of the revised Terms. Existing confirmed Bookings are not affected.

You are not entitled to any retroactive financial redress, refunds, or compensation based on differences between prior and updated Terms.

## Where to Find the Current Terms

The current version of the Booker Terms of Service is always available at [about.haveaspot.com/legal/booker-terms-of-service](https://about.haveaspot.com/legal/booker-terms-of-service). The effective date and version number are displayed at the top of the document.

## Questions?

If you have questions about a change to our Terms, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com).
