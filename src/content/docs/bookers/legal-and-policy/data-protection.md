Haveaspot takes your privacy seriously. We collect and process your personal data in accordance with our **Privacy Policy**, which explains in full what data we hold, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights.

## What Data Do We Collect?

When you use Haveaspot, we collect personal data that you provide to us — such as your name, email address, and payment information — as well as data generated through your use of the Platform, such as booking history and communications with Venues.

## How Do We Use Your Data?

We use your data to:

- Provide and operate the Platform
- Process your bookings and payments
- Send you booking confirmations, updates, and notifications
- Respond to your support queries
- Comply with our legal obligations

## Who Has Access to Your Data?

Your data may be shared with:

- **Venues** — to facilitate the bookings you make with them
- **Stripe** — our payment processor, who handles your payment data securely
- **Other third-party service providers** — as necessary to operate the Platform, all of whom are subject to appropriate data protection obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Your Rights

As a data subject under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have rights including:

- The right to **access** your personal data
- The right to **correct** inaccurate data
- The right to **delete** your data (subject to legal obligations)
- The right to **restrict** or **object to** certain processing
- The right to **data portability**

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com).

## Where to Find the Full Privacy Policy

For the complete details of how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy at [about.haveaspot.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://about.haveaspot.com/legal/privacy-policy).

By using the Platform, you consent to the processing of your personal data as described in the Privacy Policy.
