To book community spaces on Haveaspot you need a registered account. Creating one is free and takes only a few minutes.

## Who Can Create an Account?

You must be at least **18 years old** and legally capable of entering into a binding contract to use the Platform and make Bookings. If you are booking on behalf of a business, company, charity, or other organisation, you must have the authority to legally bind that entity to our terms.

## What Information Do I Need to Register?

During registration you will be asked to provide:

- Your name
- A valid email address
- A password

You agree to provide **accurate, current, and complete information** during sign-up. Inaccurate information can lead to issues with your account, bookings, or refunds.

## Keeping Your Details Up to Date

It is your responsibility to keep your account information current. If your email address, payment details, or other information changes, please update your account as soon as possible. Important notifications — including booking confirmations, refund updates, and changes to our Terms — are sent to the email address on your account.

## Booking as a Business

If you are making bookings on behalf of a business or organisation, you should indicate this during the booking process. Business Bookers may have different rights and responsibilities under our Terms — see [Governing Law and Your Rights](/bookers/legal-and-policy/governing-law/) for more detail.

## Need Help?

If you experience any issues creating your account, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com).
