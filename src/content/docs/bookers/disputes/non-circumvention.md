title: Can I Book Directly With a Venue I Found on Haveaspot?
description: Why arranging bookings outside the Platform is not permitted and the consequences of doing so.

No. If you discover a venue through Haveaspot, you must make any bookings and payments for that venue **through the Platform**. Arranging bookings or payments directly with a Venue to avoid the Haveaspot Booking Fee is not permitted under our Terms.

## Why Does This Rule Exist?

Haveaspot provides the Platform free of charge to community venues. The Haveaspot Booking Fee — charged to Bookers — is how we sustain the service. Circumventing this fee by taking transactions off-platform would undermine the model that allows us to support community spaces at no cost to them.

## What Counts as Circumvention?

Circumvention includes:

- Contacting a Venue you found on Haveaspot to arrange a booking by phone, email, or any other direct channel
- Making a payment to a Venue outside of the Haveaspot Platform specifically to avoid the Booking Fee
- Encouraging a Venue to remove their listing and deal with you directly

## What Are the Consequences?

If we determine that you have circumvented the Platform, we may:

- Suspend or permanently terminate your account
- Take further action as permitted under our Terms

## Is It Ever Acceptable to Contact a Venue Directly?

You may communicate with a Venue outside of the Platform for legitimate reasons — for example, if you already had a pre-existing relationship with the venue before discovering them on Haveaspot, or for purposes unrelated to booking. The prohibition applies specifically to **arranging bookings and payments** in order to avoid the Platform fee.

If you are unsure, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com).
