Haveaspot reserves the right to suspend or terminate your account immediately and without prior notice in certain circumstances.

## Grounds for Suspension or Termination

Your account may be suspended or closed if:

- **You materially breach our Terms** — including misrepresenting your event, circumventing the Platform, or engaging in prohibited conduct
- **You provide inaccurate or fraudulent information** — during registration, in a Booking Request, or at any other point
- **Your conduct poses a risk** — to Haveaspot, to Venues, to other Bookers, or to third parties
- **We are required to do so by law** — or if we believe it is necessary for security reasons

## What Happens to Your Bookings?

If your account is terminated by Haveaspot:

- **Pending Booking Requests** are immediately cancelled
- **Confirmed future Bookings** are automatically treated as cancelled as of the effective date of termination
- **Refunds** for cancelled future Bookings are processed in accordance with the [standard cancellation policy](/bookers/cancellations/refund-tiers/) — this means the Haveaspot Booking Fee is non-refundable, and any Hire Cost refund depends on the notice given relative to each Booking's scheduled start time

## What Survives Termination?

Even after your account is closed, certain obligations continue to apply — including any payments owed for services already received, disclaimers, limitations of liability, dispute resolution terms, data protection obligations, and the governing law provisions.

## If You Believe the Suspension Is a Mistake

If you believe your account has been suspended or terminated in error, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com) as soon as possible with a clear explanation. We will review the matter and respond accordingly.
