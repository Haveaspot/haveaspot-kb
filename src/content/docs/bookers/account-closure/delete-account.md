You can stop using Haveaspot and request the deletion of your account at any time. There are no penalties or exit fees for closing your account.

## How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account in one of two ways:

### Option 1 — Delete via the platform

Go to **Booker Dashboard → Profile → Delete Account** to delete your account directly.

### Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com) using the email address associated with your account so we can verify your identity and process your request.

## What Happens to Your Bookings?

When your account is closed, the following applies automatically:

- **Pending Booking Requests** — any requests that have not yet been confirmed by a Venue are immediately cancelled. No payment is captured.
- **Confirmed future Bookings** — any Bookings for future dates are automatically treated as **cancelled by you** as of the date your account is closed.
- **Refunds for future Bookings** — refunds are calculated strictly in accordance with the [standard cancellation policy](/bookers/cancellations/refund-tiers/), based on how much notice is given relative to each Booking's scheduled start time. The Haveaspot Booking Fee is non-refundable in most cases.

## Access to Booking History

Once your account is closed, you will lose access to your booking history and any associated information stored on the Platform. If you need a record of your past bookings for any reason, we recommend downloading or noting relevant details before submitting your deletion request.

## Your Data

We handle your personal data in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](https://about.haveaspot.com/legal/privacy-policy). Closing your account does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of all your data — some information may be retained for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. Our Privacy Policy explains what data we hold, for how long, and why.

## Changed Your Mind?

If you simply want to take a break from the Platform, you do not need to delete your account — you can just stop using it. Your account will remain in place until you choose to use it again or request deletion.
