When you make a booking on Haveaspot, the **Total Fee** is the full amount you pay. It is made up of three components.

## The Three Components

### 1. The Hire Cost
This is the fee set by the Venue for the use of their space for your chosen time and date. Venues set their own rates. If the Venue is VAT registered, their Hire Cost will already include VAT.

### 2. The Haveaspot Booking Fee
This is the fee charged by Haveaspot for providing the Platform and facilitating your Booking. It is calculated at **15% of the Hire Cost**. The Booking Fee includes VAT where this is required under UK law.

### 3. VAT on the Haveaspot Booking Fee
VAT applicable to the Haveaspot Booking Fee is included within the Booking Fee figure shown at checkout.

## When Is the Total Fee Displayed?

The Total Fee — broken down by its components — is shown clearly before you submit your Booking Request. By submitting your request, you agree to pay the Total Fee displayed at that time.

## Who Receives What?

- The **Hire Cost** goes directly to the Venue (processed via Stripe)
- The **Haveaspot Booking Fee** goes to Haveaspot for providing the Platform

Haveaspot does not take any commission from the Venue's Hire Cost.

## Is the Booking Fee Refundable?

This depends on the circumstances:

- If the Venue **declines or does not respond** to your request, no charge is made and the pre-authorisation hold is released
- If you cancel within the **48-hour grace period**, the Booking Fee is refunded minus a small Cancellation Administration Fee
- If you cancel outside the grace period, the Booking Fee is **non-refundable** in most cases

See [What Refund Will I Receive If I Cancel?](/bookers/cancellations/refund-tiers/) for the full breakdown.
