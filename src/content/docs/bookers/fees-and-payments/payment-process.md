Haveaspot uses a two-stage payment process to protect both Bookers and Venues. Your card is not charged at the point of submitting a Booking Request — it is only charged once the Venue confirms your booking.

## Stage 1 — Pre-Authorisation Hold

When you submit a Booking Request, Haveaspot (via Stripe, our payment processor) places a **pre-authorisation hold** on your chosen payment method for the full Total Fee.

This hold:
- **Reserves** the funds to ensure they are available
- Does **not** transfer money to Haveaspot or the Venue
- Appears on your bank statement as a pending transaction, but is not a charge

You need to ensure your payment method has sufficient funds available to cover the full Total Fee at the point of submitting your request.

## Stage 2 — Payment Capture

Payment is **captured** (i.e. your card is actually charged) only when the Venue confirms your Booking.

## If Your Request Is Not Confirmed

If your Booking Request is:
- **Declined** by the Venue
- **Not responded to** before the request expires
- **Cancelled by you** before confirmation (where permitted)

...the pre-authorisation hold is released and no payment is taken. The time it takes for the funds to become available in your account again depends on your **bank or card provider** — this is outside Haveaspot's control.

## Payment Security

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. Haveaspot does not store your full card details. See [Who Processes My Payment?](/bookers/fees-and-payments/stripe-payments/) for more information.
