When you make a booking through Haveaspot, you take on a number of important responsibilities — both to the Venue and to the community space you are hiring. Here is an overview of what is expected of you.

## Use the Space Lawfully

You must use the Listed Premises in compliance with **all applicable laws, regulations, and local ordinances**. This includes but is not limited to health and safety requirements, licensing laws, and any local restrictions that may apply to your type of event.

## Follow the House Rules and Venue Terms

You must review and abide by the Venue's **House Rules** and any specific Venue terms and conditions that were in effect at the time your Booking was confirmed. These are accessible at any time via the **My Bookings** section of your account. See [What Are House Rules?](/bookers/booking/house-rules/) for more detail.

## Use the Space for Its Stated Purpose

You must use the Listed Premises **only for the purpose described in your Booking Request**. Any use that is illegal, hazardous, offensive, or substantially different from what you stated when booking is not permitted.

## Be Responsible for Your Guests

You are responsible for the **conduct of all guests, attendees, and other individuals** you invite to or allow into the space during your Booking. Any breach of the rules or damage caused by your guests is your responsibility.

## Leave the Space in Good Condition

You must leave the Listed Premises in **substantially the same condition** as you found it (reasonable wear and tear excepted). You are liable for any damage caused beyond normal use.

## Communicate Respectfully

You must use the Platform's communication tools only for legitimate, booking-related purposes. Spam, abusive messages, harassing or offensive communications, and any attempt to share unnecessary personal information or circumvent the Platform are all prohibited.

## Do Not Arrange Bookings Off-Platform

You must not contact Venues you discovered through Haveaspot to arrange bookings or payments outside the Platform. See [Can I Book Directly With a Venue?](/bookers/disputes/non-circumvention/) for more detail.

## Provide Accurate Information

You must provide honest and accurate information throughout the booking process — from your Booking Request through to how you use the space on the day. Misrepresentations can result in your Booking being cancelled or your event being terminated without a refund.
