Yes. As the Booker, you are fully responsible for everyone you invite to or allow into the Listed Premises during your Booking period.

## Who Counts as Your Responsibility?

Your responsibility extends to:

- **Guests and attendees** at your event
- **Service providers** you bring to the venue (caterers, photographers, entertainers, etc.)
- **Any other individuals** you allow access to the space during your Booking

## What Are You Liable For?

You are liable for:

- Any **breach of the Venue's House Rules** by your guests
- Any **damage to the property or its contents** caused by you or your guests beyond normal wear and tear
- Any **illegal, hazardous, or disruptive conduct** by anyone present under your Booking

## Can the Venue Ask My Guests to Leave?

Yes. The Venue has the right to terminate your Booking and require everyone to vacate the premises immediately — without a refund — if the conduct of you or your guests is in serious breach of the rules, causes safety risks, or constitutes a nuisance. See [Can a Venue End My Event Early?](/bookers/your-responsibilities/venue-termination/) for more detail.

## Practical Tips

- Make sure all attendees are aware of the House Rules before the event
- For larger events, consider designating someone to help manage guests' behaviour
- If you are bringing service providers, brief them on the Venue's expectations in advance
- If something goes wrong during the event, address it quickly and communicate with the Venue

## Damage Caused by Guests

If damage is caused, see [What Counts as Damage and What Happens If I Cause It?](/bookers/your-responsibilities/damage/) for guidance on next steps and your liability.
