You are required to leave the Listed Premises in substantially the same condition as you found it. You are liable for any damage caused beyond **normal wear and tear**.

## What Is Normal Wear and Tear?

Normal wear and tear refers to the minor, expected deterioration that happens through ordinary use — for example:

- Small scuffs on walls from chairs or tables
- Minor carpet wear in high-traffic areas
- Light surface marks on furniture

These would not typically be considered damage you are liable for.

## What Counts as Damage?

Damage beyond normal wear and tear includes things such as:

- Broken furniture, fittings, or equipment
- Stains on carpets, walls, or upholstery that require cleaning or replacement
- Damage to structural elements of the building
- Graffiti or markings that require repainting
- Broken windows, doors, or locks
- Loss or theft of the Venue's property

This list is not exhaustive — the Venue's House Rules may also define what they consider to be damage or misuse of their space.

## Your Liability

You are financially liable for the **cost of repairing or replacing** anything damaged beyond normal wear and tear, whether the damage was caused by you or by your guests or attendees.

## If Damage Occurs

If damage happens during your Booking period:

1. **Report it to the Venue immediately** — do not wait until after you have left
2. **Document the damage** where possible (photographs are helpful)
3. Work with the Venue to agree on the appropriate remedy

## Disputes Over Damage

If a Venue raises a claim for damage that you dispute, try to resolve it directly with them first. If you cannot reach an agreement, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com) for guidance. Haveaspot may offer non-binding assistance but is not a party to the direct agreement between you and the Venue.
