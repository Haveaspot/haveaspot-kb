Yes. A Venue has the right to terminate your Booking during the hire period and require you and all attendees to immediately vacate the Listed Premises. This is a right they retain regardless of how much of your hire time remains.

## When Can a Venue Terminate Your Booking?

A Venue can end your event early if they reasonably determine that any of the following apply:

- **You misrepresented your event** — for example, you stated a small private gathering but arrived with a much larger crowd or a very different type of event
- **You or your guests are in breach of the rules** — this includes a serious breach of the House Rules, these Terms, or applicable laws
- **There is a safety risk** — if your event poses a significant risk to the safety or security of the premises, Venue staff, other occupants, or neighbours
- **Your event is causing a nuisance** — if the event is causing undue interference or disturbance that the Venue considers unacceptable

## Is There a Refund If My Event Is Terminated?

If a Venue terminates your Booking because of any of the above, you are **not entitled to a refund** for the unused portion of your hire time.

## How to Avoid This

- Be accurate and honest in your Booking Request about the nature and scale of your event
- Ensure all guests and attendees are aware of and respect the House Rules before arrival
- Address any issues that arise during the event quickly and constructively
- Cooperate with Venue staff if they raise concerns during the hire

If you feel a Venue has acted unreasonably in terminating your event, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com). Haveaspot may offer non-binding assistance, though we are not a party to the direct agreement between you and the Venue.
