Haveaspot offers a short **grace period** after a Booking is confirmed, allowing you to cancel and receive a near-full refund if you made an accidental booking or changed your mind quickly.

## How It Works

If you cancel a confirmed Booking within **exactly 48 hours of the Venue confirming it**, you will receive a full refund of the Total Fee minus a **Cancellation Administration Fee**.

The Cancellation Administration Fee is set at:
> **3% of the Total Fee + £0.30 (inclusive of VAT, where applicable)**

This fee covers the non-refundable third-party payment processing costs that Haveaspot incurs on the transaction and cannot be waived.

## The 14-Day Exception

The grace period does **not apply** if the scheduled start time of your event is **less than 14 days away** from the time the Booking was confirmed.

This exception exists to protect Venues' calendars — a last-minute cancellation close to the event date would prevent the Venue from finding another booking to fill that slot.

## Summary

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Cancelled within 48 hours of confirmation, event is 14+ days away | Full refund minus Cancellation Administration Fee (3% + £0.30) |
| Cancelled within 48 hours of confirmation, but event is less than 14 days away | Standard cancellation policy applies — grace period does not apply |

## After the Grace Period

If you cancel after the 48-hour window has closed, the standard cancellation tiers apply. See [What Refund Will I Receive If I Cancel?](/bookers/cancellations/refund-tiers/) for the full breakdown.
