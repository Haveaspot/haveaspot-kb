Haveaspot's cancellation policy sets out your **contractual rights** regarding refunds. These tiers are the minimum you are entitled to. However, in some circumstances, a Venue may choose to be more generous.

## Discretionary Refunds

A Venue may, entirely at their own discretion, choose to offer you a **higher or full refund** in cases of extenuating circumstances — for example, if you have a genuine emergency, a serious illness, or another compelling reason for cancelling.

This is entirely the Venue's decision and is made:
- **At their sole discretion** — they are under no obligation to do so
- **At their own expense** — the Venue bears the financial cost of any amount refunded beyond the standard policy

## These Refunds Cannot Be Demanded

A discretionary refund from a Venue is a goodwill gesture. It is **not a right** under Haveaspot's Terms and cannot be:
- Demanded or expected as a matter of course
- Appealed to Haveaspot if the Venue declines to offer one
- Guaranteed for any particular type of circumstance

## How to Request a Discretionary Refund

If you believe your circumstances are exceptional, you can:
1. Contact the Venue directly through the Platform messaging system
2. Explain your situation and ask if they would consider a higher refund

Haveaspot encourages Venues to be considerate when genuine hardship is involved, but we cannot compel a Venue to act outside the standard policy.

## Your Standard Rights Always Apply

Regardless of whether a Venue offers a discretionary refund, your standard cancellation rights under our policy always apply. See [What Refund Will I Receive If I Cancel?](/bookers/cancellations/refund-tiers/) for the full breakdown.
