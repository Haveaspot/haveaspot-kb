If your plans change and you need a different date, you may be able to reschedule your Booking rather than cancel it.

## How to Request a Reschedule

You can submit a **reschedule request** through the Platform up to **14 days before the scheduled start time** of your existing Booking. Requests made within 14 days of the event cannot be submitted through the reschedule process.

To reschedule:
1. Log in to your Haveaspot account
2. Go to **My Bookings**
3. Select the Booking you wish to reschedule
4. Follow the on-screen steps to propose a new date

## If the Venue Approves the New Date

If the Venue accepts your proposed new date, **100% of your Total Fee** — including both the Hire Cost and the Haveaspot Booking Fee — is carried over to the new Booking. No additional fees apply.

## If the Venue Cannot Accommodate the New Date

If the Venue declines your reschedule request or is unavailable on your proposed date, you have two options:

- **Keep your original date** — your Booking remains in place as confirmed
- **Cancel your Booking** — standard cancellation terms apply based on how much notice you give. See [What Refund Will I Receive If I Cancel?](/bookers/cancellations/refund-tiers/)

## Can I Reschedule More Than Once?

Rescheduling is subject to Venue availability and approval each time. There is no guaranteed right to reschedule any confirmed Booking.

## Need Help?

If you are having difficulty submitting a reschedule request, contact us at [support@haveaspot.com](mailto:support@haveaspot.com).
