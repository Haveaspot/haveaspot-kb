If you cancel a confirmed Booking after the [48-hour grace period](/bookers/cancellations/grace-period/) has expired (or if the grace period does not apply), your refund is determined by how much notice you give before the scheduled start time of your event.

## Cancellation Refund Tiers

### More Than 14 Days' Notice

If you cancel **more than 14 days before** the scheduled start time:

- **Hire Cost:** 100% refund
- **Haveaspot Booking Fee:** Refunded minus the Cancellation Administration Fee (3% of the Total Fee + £0.30)

### Between 7 and 14 Days' Notice

If you cancel **between 7 and 14 days (inclusive) before** the scheduled start time:

- **Hire Cost:** 50% refund
- **Haveaspot Booking Fee:** 100% non-refundable

### Less Than 7 Days' Notice

If you cancel **less than 7 days before** the scheduled start time:

- **Hire Cost:** No refund (0%)
- **Haveaspot Booking Fee:** 100% non-refundable

## Summary Table

| Notice Given | Hire Cost Refund | Haveaspot Booking Fee |
|---|---|---|
| More than 14 days | 100% | Refunded minus 3% + £0.30 |
| 7–14 days | 50% | Non-refundable |
| Less than 7 days | 0% | Non-refundable |

## Important Notes

- The notice period is measured from the time you cancel to the **scheduled start time** of the event — not the date of the Booking or the date of confirmation
- Refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Stripe; processing times vary by bank
- In some cases, a Venue may choose to offer you a higher refund at their own discretion — this is never a guaranteed right. See [Can a Venue Offer Me a Better Refund?](/bookers/cancellations/discretionary-refund/)

## Not Yet Past the Grace Period?

If your Booking was confirmed within the last 48 hours and the event is more than 14 days away, a more favourable grace period may apply. See [What Is the 48-Hour Grace Period?](/bookers/cancellations/grace-period/).
