Making a booking on Haveaspot involves a simple request-and-confirm process. Here is how it works from start to finish.

## Step 1 — Find a Space

Browse the Platform to find a Listed Premises that suits your needs. Each listing shows the venue's description, available dates, House Rules, and pricing. Take time to read the listing carefully before submitting a request.

## Step 2 — Submit a Booking Request

When you have found a suitable space, submit a **Booking Request** through the Platform. You will need to provide details about your intended use of the space, including the nature of your event. See [What Information Do I Need to Provide?](/bookers/booking/booking-request-information/) for guidance on what to include.

**Important:** Submitting a Booking Request does not guarantee a Booking. It is a request that the Venue must review and approve.

## Step 3 — The Venue Reviews Your Request

Once submitted, your request is sent to the Venue. The Venue is responsible for reviewing it and deciding whether to **confirm or decline**. All bookings on Haveaspot require Venue confirmation — there is no automatic or instant booking process.

During this time, a **pre-authorisation hold** is placed on your payment method for the Total Fee. This reserves the funds but does not charge you. See [How Does Payment Work?](/bookers/fees-and-payments/payment-process/) for more detail.

## Step 4 — Booking Confirmed

Once the Venue confirms your request, your Booking is formed and payment is captured. You will receive a confirmation notification and can view your booking details at any time under **My Bookings** in your account.

At this point, you have entered into a direct agreement with the Venue for the hire of the space. Haveaspot is not a party to that agreement but our Terms (including the Cancellation Policy) always take precedence over the Venue's own rules.

## Step 5 — Your Event

On the day, ensure you and your guests comply with the Venue's House Rules and leave the space in the condition you found it. See [What Are My Responsibilities as a Booker?](/bookers/your-responsibilities/overview/) for the full picture.

## If Your Request Is Declined or Expires

If the Venue declines your request, or if it expires without a response, the pre-authorisation hold on your payment will be released. The time it takes for those funds to become available again depends on your bank or card provider.
