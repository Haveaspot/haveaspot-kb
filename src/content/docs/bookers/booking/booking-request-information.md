When you submit a Booking Request on Haveaspot, you are asking a Venue to confirm a reservation. Providing accurate and complete information is essential — both for the Venue to make an informed decision, and to protect your booking.

## What to Include

Your Booking Request should clearly describe:

- **The nature of your event** — for example, a children's party, fitness class, community meeting, or corporate workshop
- **The number of attendees** you expect
- **Any specific requirements** relevant to the space or the Venue's listing
- **Any information requested by the Venue** in their listing

## Why Accuracy Matters

You are required to provide **true, accurate, and complete** information in your Booking Request. Material misrepresentations — such as significantly understating the number of attendees or misrepresenting the type of event — can have serious consequences:

- The Venue or Haveaspot may **cancel your Booking without a refund**
- The Venue may **terminate your event mid-hire** and require you and your guests to leave the premises immediately, also without refund
- Your account may be suspended or terminated

## Business Bookers

If you are making a booking on behalf of a business, company, charity, or other organisation, you must indicate this during the booking process and confirm you have authority to act on that entity's behalf.

## After You Submit

Once submitted, your Booking Request is reviewed by the Venue. You cannot change a Booking Request after submission — if you need to make a significant change, you may need to withdraw your request (if it has not yet been confirmed) and submit a new one.

If your event details change significantly after a Booking is confirmed, contact the Venue directly through the Platform as soon as possible.
