Yes. As a Venue on Haveaspot, **you are responsible for deciding whether to accept a booking request** and for taking any steps to verify or vet the Booker that you feel are appropriate.

## What Haveaspot does not do

Haveaspot is an intermediary marketplace. It **does not**:

- Carry out identity checks on Bookers
- Conduct background checks
- Verify the accuracy of information a Booker provides about themselves or their event
- Guarantee the suitability of any Booker for your venue

This is stated clearly in the Venue Service Agreement. Because Haveaspot is not a party to the contract between you and the Booker, the decision to accept a booking — and the consequences of that decision — rest with you.

## What you can do

Before confirming a booking, you can:

- **Review the booking request carefully** — check the event type, number of guests, and any notes provided by the Booker
- **Ask questions via the platform** — use Haveaspot's messaging tools to request more information about the event before confirming
- **Decline requests that concern you** — you are not obligated to accept every booking request
- **Set clear House Rules** — well-written House Rules help filter out unsuitable Bookers and give you a basis for ending a booking early if rules are breached. See [How to Write Accurate and Effective House Rules](/spots/managing-your-listing/house-rules/)

## Why this matters

Because Haveaspot does not vet Bookers, any risk associated with who uses your space is primarily yours to manage. If a Booker causes damage, creates a nuisance, or acts unlawfully at your venue, Haveaspot cannot be held responsible — the obligation to have adequate safeguards (including insurance and due diligence) lies with you.

See [What Insurance Do I Need to List on Haveaspot?](/spots/legal/insurance/) for guidance on protecting your venue.

## Using your judgement

Most bookings on Haveaspot are straightforward and problem-free. But being a responsible Venue means asking the right questions upfront and not confirming bookings you are not comfortable with. It is better to decline a request than to deal with a difficult situation on the day.
