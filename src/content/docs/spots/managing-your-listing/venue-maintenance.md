As a Venue on Haveaspot, you are responsible for ensuring that your space is safe, accurately described, and fit for the bookings you accept. These responsibilities sit entirely with you — Haveaspot does not inspect or audit listed venues.

## Keeping your space safe

You must ensure your premises are **safe for Bookers and their guests** at all times during bookings. This includes:

- Maintaining the structural integrity and cleanliness of the space
- Ensuring all equipment, furniture, and facilities you provide are in safe working order
- Addressing any hazards promptly — particularly before a booking takes place
- Complying with all applicable health and safety legislation, fire safety regulations, and building codes

If your space becomes unsafe or unavailable due to a maintenance issue, you should not allow bookings to proceed. If a confirmed booking is affected, contact Haveaspot at support@haveaspot.com as soon as possible.

## Keeping your listing accurate

Your listing must **accurately reflect your space** at all times. If something changes — the layout, available facilities, capacity, or access arrangements — you must update your listing promptly.

Inaccurate listings lead to disappointed Bookers, disputes, and negative reviews. In serious cases, a misleading listing could result in action against your account.

## Insurance

You must maintain adequate **public liability insurance** throughout your time on the platform. This is not something Haveaspot arranges — it is your independent responsibility. See [What Insurance Do I Need to List on Haveaspot?](/spots/legal/insurance/).

## Surveillance equipment

If you have security cameras, they must be disclosed in your listing. Cameras are never permitted in bathrooms or changing areas. See [Do I Need to Disclose Security Cameras at My Venue?](/spots/managing-your-listing/surveillance-disclosure/).

## Your ongoing responsibilities

In summary, as a Venue you are expected to:

- Keep the space clean, safe, and operational
- Update your listing whenever details change
- Respond promptly to Booker enquiries about the space
- Honour confirmed bookings where your space is in a suitable condition to do so
