There are limited circumstances in which you are entitled to end a booking before it has concluded and ask the Booker to leave your premises. This is distinct from a cancellation — it applies to bookings that have already started or are under way.

## When you can end a booking early

You may legitimately terminate a booking in progress if:

- The Booker **misrepresented the nature of the event** when making the booking (for example, describing a small private gathering but arriving with a much larger group or for a different type of event)
- The Booker or their guests are creating a **safety risk** to themselves, your staff, your property, or others
- The Booker is in **serious breach of your House Rules**
- The Booker's conduct is **interfering with your operations** or those of other occupants of the building

These are genuine, material grounds — not minor inconveniences. If you end a booking early without proper justification, you could face a complaint from the Booker and potential liability.

## What happens with the refund?

If you end a booking early on any of the above grounds, **no refund is due to the Booker**. The Booker forfeits the Hire Cost for the session.

## How to handle it in practice

If you need to ask a Booker to leave:

1. **Speak calmly and clearly** — explain which rule or issue has led to the decision
2. **Document the situation** — note the time, what happened, and if possible take photographs
3. **Contact Haveaspot** — inform support@haveaspot.com as soon as possible so the situation is on record
4. **Do not engage in a physical confrontation** — if there is a risk to safety, call emergency services if necessary

## Important: do not misuse this right

Ending a booking early inappropriately — for example, because you have a better booking opportunity or you have changed your mind — is not a valid use of this provision. In that situation, it would be treated as a Venue cancellation with the associated financial consequences. See [What Happens If I Cancel a Confirmed Booking?](/spots/cancellations/venue-cancels/).
