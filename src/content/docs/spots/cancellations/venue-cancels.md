Cancelling a confirmed booking as a Venue has serious consequences — both for the Booker and for your account. It is important to understand these before taking any action.

## What happens when you cancel

If you cancel a confirmed booking:

- The Booker receives a **100% refund of the Total Fee** they paid — this includes both the Hire Cost and the Haveaspot Booking Fee
- You become liable to pay Haveaspot the **Haveaspot Booking Fee plus VAT** — this is a direct financial obligation to Haveaspot, not the Booker

In short, a venue cancellation costs you the Booking Fee, even though you received no income from the booking.

## How Haveaspot recovers the Booking Fee

Haveaspot will recover the outstanding Booking Fee (plus VAT) through the following steps:

1. **Automatic debit** — Haveaspot will first attempt to debit the amount directly from your Stripe Connect Account
2. **Bank account debit** — if the refund to the Booker causes a negative balance on your Stripe account, Stripe is authorised under your Stripe Connected Account Agreement to debit your linked bank account directly to recover that shortfall
3. **Right of set-off** — Haveaspot may also withhold any amounts owed from your future payouts
4. **Direct invoice** — if the above steps are insufficient, Haveaspot will issue you a direct invoice payable within **30 days**
5. **Debt recovery** — failure to pay an invoice may result in suspension of your account and formal debt recovery action

## Why such strict consequences?

Bookers rely on confirmed bookings to plan their events. A last-minute cancellation by a Venue can cause significant disruption and harm Haveaspot's reputation as a reliable platform. The cancellation policy is designed to incentivise Venues to honour their confirmed bookings.

## Can I ever terminate a booking once it has started?

There are limited circumstances in which you can **end a booking early** — for example, if the Booker has misrepresented their event, is creating a safety risk, or is in serious breach of your House Rules. This is different from a cancellation and is handled separately. See [Can I End a Booking Early or Ask a Booker to Leave?](/spots/cancellations/ending-booking-early/).

## What about repeated cancellations?

Haveaspot monitors Venue conduct on the platform. A pattern of cancellations — particularly at short notice — may result in your listing being suspended or your account being terminated.

## Before you cancel

If you are facing a situation that may force you to cancel — such as a maintenance issue or unforeseen closure — contact Haveaspot at support@haveaspot.com as early as possible. The sooner you raise the issue, the better the outcome is likely to be for all parties.
