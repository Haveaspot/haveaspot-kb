Understanding how fees work on Haveaspot helps you set the right price for your space and know exactly what you will receive from each booking.

## The three figures to know

### Hire Cost

The **Hire Cost** is the price you set for the use of your space. This is the amount you will receive when a booking is paid.

**Important:** if you are VAT registered, your Hire Cost must be set **inclusive of VAT**. You are responsible for accounting for and paying any VAT due to HMRC — Haveaspot does not handle this on your behalf.

### Haveaspot Booking Fee

Haveaspot charges Bookers a **Booking Fee** of **15% of your Hire Cost**. This fee is paid by the Booker — it is added on top of your Hire Cost when they check out. You do **not** pay this fee, and it does not reduce the amount you receive.

**Listing your space on Haveaspot is free.** There are no fees charged to you as a Venue for using the platform.

### Total Fee

The **Total Fee** is what the Booker pays in full — your Hire Cost plus the Haveaspot Booking Fee. This is the figure Bookers see when they complete their booking.

## A simple example

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Your Hire Cost | £200 |
| Haveaspot Booking Fee — 15% (paid by Booker) | £30 |
| **Total Fee (paid by Booker)** | **£230** |
| **Your payout** | **£200** |

*The above is illustrative only.*

## What happens if a refund is issued?

If a Booker cancels and is entitled to a refund of the Hire Cost, that refund is processed back to them. However, the **Haveaspot Booking Fee is non-refundable to you** — meaning if you cancel a booking, you will owe Haveaspot the Booking Fee plus VAT. See [What Happens If I Cancel a Confirmed Booking?](/spots/cancellations/venue-cancels/) for more detail.

If you issue a **discretionary refund** beyond what the cancellation policy requires, you are still liable for the Booking Fee. See [Can I Issue a Discretionary Refund to a Booker?](/spots/cancellations/discretionary-refund/).
