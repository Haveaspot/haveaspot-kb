Once a Booker confirms and pays for a booking, you will receive the **Hire Cost** directly to your connected Stripe account. Here is how it works and what to expect.

## How money flows

1. The Booker pays the **Total Fee** (your Hire Cost plus the Haveaspot Booking Fee) at the time of booking
2. Stripe processes the payment
3. The **Hire Cost** portion is transferred to your Stripe Connect Account
4. Stripe then pays out your balance to your linked bank account

Haveaspot does **not** hold your funds at any point. Money moves from the Booker directly through Stripe to you.

## Payout timings

Payout timings are controlled by **Stripe**, not Haveaspot. As a general guide:

- **Your first payout**: typically arrives around **7 days** after the payment is made
- **Subsequent payouts**: typically arrive within **2–7 days** of a payment being processed

These timings may vary depending on Stripe's processes, your account status, and your bank. If you are experiencing delays, you will need to check your Stripe dashboard or contact Stripe support directly — Haveaspot is not able to expedite payouts on your behalf.

## Setting up your bank account

Your payout bank account is configured within your **Stripe Connect Account**. Make sure your bank details are correct and up to date in Stripe to avoid any delays.

## What can affect my payout?

There are a few situations that may affect the amount you receive or delay a payout:

- **Refunds**: if a Booker is entitled to a refund, this will reduce the amount paid out to you. See [Understanding the Haveaspot Cancellation Policy](/spots/cancellations/cancellation-policy/) for refund rules.
- **Chargebacks**: if a Booker disputes a payment with their bank, Stripe may withhold or reverse the relevant funds. See [What Happens If a Booker Initiates a Chargeback?](/spots/payments/chargebacks/).
- **Set-off**: if you owe Haveaspot any fees (for example, due to a venue cancellation), Haveaspot may deduct these from future payouts.
- **Insufficient funds**: if a refund is required but your Stripe balance is too low to cover it, additional steps may be needed. See [What Happens If My Stripe Account Has Insufficient Funds for a Refund?](/spots/payments/insufficient-funds/).

## Need help with a payout?

If you believe there is an error in your payout, start by checking your **Stripe dashboard** for a transaction breakdown. If you believe the issue is on Haveaspot's side, contact us at support@haveaspot.com.
