When you add someone to your Haveaspot account, you assign them a **role** that determines what they can access and do. There are two roles on the platform: **Owner** and **Member**.

## Owner

The **Owner** role has **full administrative access** to your account. This includes everything a Member can do, plus:

- Access to **billing and payment settings**, including your Stripe Connect Account configuration
- The ability to **add, manage, and remove other Team Members**
- The ability to **close the account**
- Full access to all account settings

There is typically one primary Owner — the person who set up the account. However, the Owner role can be assigned to additional trusted individuals if needed.

**Be careful who you make an Owner.** Because this role includes access to billing and payment settings, it carries significant trust. Only assign the Owner role to people who genuinely need this level of access.

## Member

The **Member** role provides day-to-day operational access without access to sensitive billing or account management features. A Member can typically:

- View and manage booking requests
- Update listing details, availability, and House Rules (subject to lock-in rules)
- Communicate with Bookers via the platform
- View booking history and upcoming bookings

Members cannot access payment settings, invite or remove other team members, or make account-level changes.

## Assigning the right role

When adding a new Team Member, think about what they actually need to do:

- **Front-of-house staff or venue coordinators** — likely suitable for the Member role
- **Business partners or senior managers who handle finances** — may need the Owner role

## Your liability

Regardless of role, **you as the account holder are responsible for all actions taken by your Team Members**. This applies whether the action was intentional, accidental, or the result of a misunderstanding. See [How to Add and Manage Team Members on Your Account](/spots/team/adding-team-members/).

If someone no longer works for you, remove their access immediately. See [What Should I Do If a Team Member Leaves?](/spots/team/revoking-access/).
