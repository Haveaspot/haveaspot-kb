If you have staff or colleagues who help manage your venue, you can give them access to your Haveaspot account as **Team Members**. This means they can log in and carry out tasks — such as reviewing booking requests, updating your listing, or responding to Bookers — without needing to use your own login credentials.

## How to add a Team Member

You can invite Team Members from your account settings. You will need to provide their email address and assign them a role. They will receive an invitation to create their own Haveaspot login linked to your venue account.

## Choosing the right role

When you add a Team Member, you will assign them a role. The role determines what they can see and do within your account. See [What Permissions Do Different Team Member Roles Have?](/spots/team/team-member-roles/) for a full breakdown.

## Your responsibility for Team Member actions

As the account holder, you are **fully responsible for everything your Team Members do on the platform**. This includes:

- Booking decisions they make on your behalf
- Changes they make to your listing or House Rules
- Any communications they send to Bookers
- Compliance with Haveaspot's terms

If a Team Member makes an error — even an accidental one — the consequences fall on your account, not theirs personally. Choose who you invite carefully, and make sure they understand the platform's rules.

## Keeping access up to date

It is your responsibility to ensure that only currently authorised individuals have access to your account. If a team member leaves your organisation, you must **revoke their access promptly**. See [What Should I Do If a Team Member Leaves?](/spots/team/revoking-access/).

## How many Team Members can I add?

There is no fixed limit specified for Team Members. If you have specific questions about large team setups, contact support@haveaspot.com.
