If you have a complaint or dispute with Haveaspot — for example, about a payout, a decision made about your account, or how a situation was handled — there is a clear process to follow. The process is designed to resolve issues fairly and as quickly as possible.

## Step 1 — Contact Haveaspot support

The first step is always to **raise the issue directly with Haveaspot** by emailing:

**support@haveaspot.com**

In your message, include:

- A clear description of the issue
- Any relevant booking references or account details
- What outcome you are seeking

Most issues can be resolved at this stage. Haveaspot aims to respond to support queries promptly and will work with you to understand the situation.

## Step 2 — Good faith negotiation

If your complaint is not resolved through the initial support contact, both parties are expected to enter a period of **good faith negotiation** lasting up to **21 days**. During this time, you and Haveaspot will try to reach a mutually acceptable resolution through discussion and correspondence.

Keep a record of all communications during this process.

## Step 3 — Mediation

If good faith negotiation does not resolve the dispute within 21 days, either party can refer the matter to **mediation**. Mediation involves an independent third party helping both sides reach a resolution, without going to court.

Mediation is typically quicker and less expensive than legal proceedings and should be attempted before escalating to the courts.

## Step 4 — Courts of England and Wales

If mediation is unsuccessful, the dispute may be referred to the **courts of England and Wales**, which have jurisdiction over matters arising under the Venue Service Agreement.

## A note on timing

Do not delay in raising a complaint. Acting promptly gives the best chance of a resolution, and delay can sometimes affect your position — particularly in relation to payment disputes or time-sensitive issues.
