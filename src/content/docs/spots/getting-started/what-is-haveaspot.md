Haveaspot is an online marketplace that connects people looking to hire a venue — known as **Bookers** — with venue owners and managers like you, known as **Venues** or **Spots**.

## What Haveaspot does

Haveaspot provides the platform where you can list your space, set your pricing and availability, publish your House Rules, and receive booking requests. When a Booker confirms a booking through the platform, payment is handled securely via **Stripe** — Haveaspot's payment processor.

In simple terms, Haveaspot gives you the tools to:

- Create and manage your venue listing
- Set your Hire Cost, availability, and House Rules
- Communicate with Bookers
- Receive payments directly to your Stripe account

## What Haveaspot does not do

It is important to understand that Haveaspot is an **intermediary** — it is not a party to the contract between you and a Booker. Once a booking is confirmed, the agreement for the use of your space is between you and the Booker directly.

This means Haveaspot:

- Does **not** guarantee or vet the Bookers who book your space
- Does **not** hold funds on your behalf
- Is **not** responsible for what happens at your venue during a booking

For more on what this means in practice, see [What Does Haveaspot's Role as an Intermediary Mean for Me?](/spots/getting-started/intermediary-role/).

## How the money works

Listing your space on Haveaspot is **free**. Haveaspot charges Bookers a **Booking Fee** on top of your Hire Cost — you do not pay this fee, the Booker does. Payments are processed by Stripe and paid out directly to your connected Stripe account.

See [How Does the Fee Structure Work?](/spots/payments/fee-structure/) for a full breakdown.

## Getting started

Before you can go live, you will need a few things in place — such as public liability insurance and, if applicable, an alcohol licence. Your Stripe Connect Account for receiving payments is set up as part of the onboarding process itself, so there is nothing to arrange separately beforehand. See [What Do I Need Before I Can Start Listing My Spot?](/spots/getting-started/what-you-need/) for a full checklist.
