Before your listing can go live and start accepting bookings, there are a few important things you need to have ready. Working through this checklist in advance will make the setup process much smoother.

## Essential requirements

### A Stripe Connect Account

You need a verified **Stripe Connect Account** to receive payments through Haveaspot — but you do not need to set this up before you start. **Stripe Connect Account setup is part of the Haveaspot onboarding process.** When you register your Spot, you will be guided through connecting with Stripe at the appropriate step.

During this process, Stripe will carry out a **Know Your Customer (KYC)** verification, which involves providing some identity and business information. This is managed entirely by Stripe, not Haveaspot. See [Setting Up Your Stripe Account](/spots/payments/stripe-setup/) for more detail on what to expect.

### Public liability insurance

You must hold **adequate public liability insurance** before listing your space. Haveaspot does not provide or arrange insurance on your behalf. See [What Insurance Do I Need to List on Haveaspot?](/spots/legal/insurance/) for more detail.

### Any required licences

If you plan to allow alcohol at your venue, you must hold all **necessary licences** before enabling that option in your listing. See [What Licences Do I Need If Alcohol Is Permitted at My Venue?](/spots/legal/alcohol-licensing/).

## Before you write your listing

### Accurate space information

Your listing must accurately describe your space — including its capacity, layout, facilities, and any restrictions. Misleading listings can lead to disputes and may put your account at risk.

### Your House Rules

Think carefully about how you want Bookers to use your space. Your **House Rules** are a key part of your listing and are legally binding for Bookers at the time of their booking confirmation. See [How to Write Accurate and Effective House Rules](/spots/managing-your-listing/house-rules/).

### Surveillance disclosure

If you have any security cameras at your venue, you are required to disclose them in your listing. Cameras in bathrooms or changing areas are strictly prohibited. See [Do I Need to Disclose Security Cameras at My Venue?](/spots/managing-your-listing/surveillance-disclosure/).

### Your Hire Cost (inclusive of VAT)

Your **Hire Cost** must be set inclusive of VAT if you are VAT registered. Haveaspot adds its Booking Fee on top for the Booker — so the price you set is what you receive (before Stripe processing). See [How Does the Fee Structure Work?](/spots/payments/fee-structure/).

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Stripe Connect Account connected (completed during onboarding)
- [ ] Public liability insurance in place
- [ ] Alcohol licences obtained (if applicable)
- [ ] Space description accurate and complete
- [ ] House Rules drafted and reviewed
- [ ] Security cameras disclosed (if any)
- [ ] Hire Cost set inclusive of VAT (if applicable)
